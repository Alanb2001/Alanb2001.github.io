A couple of weeks after graduating from university, I got to work at Psycho Hound/Roughneck Studios as a freelance junior game programmer. I worked with them for three months before leaving, and it taught me a lot about working in the industry and a lot about networking with Unreal Engine while making gamplay features.

I had a great time working for the company and learned a lot while there. My main role was to make gameplay mechanics for two multiplayer games. Most of my time was spent making gamplay features for a game that was being made that was similar to Dead by Daylight. One mechanic that I made was to be able to hide in lockers, and while making this, I had to keep in mind that I would have to replicate the gamplay mechanic so it would look right to other players near by. Near the end of my time with the company, I worked on Ruff ‘N’ Tumble to make extra minigames with the other programmers. Here is a link to the <a href="https://store.steampowered.com/app/1190740/Ruff_N_Tumble_Mayhem/">Steam page </a> for the game.


The next role I got after that was with INFINITY 27. It started off as a one-month bootcamp to make an enemy for their game Samsara. After that month, I got a freelance contract with them to polish up and finish the enemy to be ready to be put into the main branch of the game so players can go against it. Currently it is in early access on <a href="https://store.epicgames.com/en-US/p/samsara-57a12a">Epic games</a> and once the full release happens, it will also be available on Steam. Currently there is a <a href="https://store.steampowered.com/app/1683570/Samsara/">Steam page</a> for the game.

Working on this project taught me a lot about time management and planning before starting work on anything so that you don't bottleneck yourself down the line. I used both C++ and blueprints to make this enemy. I joined the project while it was already a few years into development, so most of the basic AI behaviour was done, such as attacking, fleeing, and chasing, so most of the work was dedicated to working on the unique aspects of my enemy, such as her three abilities. While I was working on the abilities, I made one of them into a C++ component to make it usable on other enemies if the other devs wanted to use it. The enemy I made was an archer-type enemy named Divine Deva Archer. She has three distinct abilities. The first one is she shoots the player three times in quick succession, and if she misses all three shots, she will then do her second ability, which is an AOE attack. Her third and final ability is when the player gets close to her, she will blind the player by turning the screen white and spawn a decoy of herself and teleport back to her spawn location.
